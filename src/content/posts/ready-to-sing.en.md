Lately, my happiness has felt very concrete.

It is not the kind of joy that flashes past when I see good news. It stays with me when everything is quiet and I catch myself smiling. A melody appears in my head while I am walking, and even the parts of the songs I have not quite nailed in rehearsal feel worth looking forward to because I am preparing for a performance.

I am going to perform.

Just thinking about it makes something light up inside me.

## Finally taking the songs out of my headphones

The stage in my imagination is always simple: the lights come up, the music starts, and I sing.

But walking all the way to the performance teaches you that it is made of many small moments. Listening to a melody until it feels familiar. Remembering where to breathe. Trying to let movement and rhythm live in the same body. Then, on an unremarkable evening, suddenly realizing: ah. I am actually about to stand up there.

The stage is an honest place. Once the lights come on, nerves, excitement, anticipation, and all the traces of private practice probably arrive together.

I do not want to pretend that I am not nervous at all.

But this time, I want to remember the happiness more than the fear. The happiness of finally taking favorite songs out of my headphones and placing them into a real room. The happiness of a voice no longer circling only around me, but meeting the lights, the air, and the people in the audience.

## These four songs brought me here

I will mainly sing four songs.

They come from two directions I love: MyGO!!!!! and hitorie. Each one has a different speed and color, and together they split my feelings into four distinct parts.

### MyGO!!!!! - "猛独侵袭"

"猛独侵袭" carries a very direct kind of force.

It is not the kind of gentle encouragement that tells you everything will be fine. It first admits that the world can leave you battered. It admits that something inside you refuses to yield. Then it still chooses to push the voice outward.

I love how it rushes forward. When I sing it, I do not want to merely hit the notes and keep time. I want to leave that feeling onstage too: even if it is not easy, we keep going.

### MyGO!!!!! - "影色舞"

If "猛独侵袭" rushes forward, then "影色舞" is more like stepping into light and shadow.

It has a beautiful but slightly dangerous sway. The emotion does not shoot straight out; it turns slowly inside the rhythm. When I sing it, I hope I will not stand too neatly. The movement does not need to be perfect, but it should follow the music and let the room breathe with it.

### hitorie - "monocolour"

"monocolour" feels like a painting with very few colors and very deep layers.

It has a calm sharpness. The melody is beautiful, but it is not tame. Singing it cannot depend only on making the voice louder. I have to hold the tone back and hand out the emotion hidden in each line a little at a time.

That is probably why it is difficult and why it is so mesmerizing.

### hitorie - "Namid[A]me"

The title "Namid[A]me" already feels like tears falling into rain.

It calls for slowing down a little and singing every word clearly. Not exaggerating the sadness on purpose, but holding emotions that have already passed, yet occasionally return, up to the light for a moment.

I hope that when it ends, it will feel as if the wind has dried everything off.

## The nerves are real too

Of course I will still be nervous.

I will worry about forgetting lyrics, rushing the beat, missing the first line, or pressing too hard in the part I love most.

But lately I have started to understand that nervousness is not the opposite of happiness. It is only reminding me that this matters to me.

So this time, I will not wish to make no mistakes.

I only want to stand steadily, hear the rhythm, and hear the room. I want every line to arrive where it belongs. Even if something is imperfect, I do not want to miss the actual moment of standing onstage because I was afraid of a flaw.

## See you there

The four songs together make a short but complete path.

From rushing forward, to moving, to slowing down, and finally to gently gathering the part of the heart where it is still raining.

They used to belong to headphones, music players, and many moments of listening alone. Now they are going to be sung in a real room. Even thinking about it feels unreal, and very happy.

If you are there too, I hope you can hear it.

Before I go onstage, I want to leave this sentence here first:

I am really happy.

I am not waiting for every detail to be perfect before allowing myself to be happy. I am carrying the nerves that have not yet faded and the parts that still need work, and I am honestly glad about this.

When the lights come up, I will sing with everything I have.

See you there.
